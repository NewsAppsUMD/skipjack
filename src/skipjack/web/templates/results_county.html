Only contests every {{ county }} voter had on the ballot are compared: statewide races, a congressional or legislative district when the county lies wholly inside one, and county offices elected countywide. The results files do not split the vote for {{ outran.top_title }} by district, so a district that covers part of the county cannot be compared fairly and is left out. So is a candidate with no opponent from another party, or in a contest with no more candidates than seats, since every voter who marked that contest could only vote for them. In a contest where voters choose several candidates, someone can also pick up votes from voters whose own party fielded fewer candidates than seats. The percentage is the lead as a share of the votes for the party's candidate for {{ outran.top_title }}.
