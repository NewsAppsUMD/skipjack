Participation among current voters

The share of today's registered voters who voted in each election, among those who could have voted: registered by election day and old enough. Primaries are closed in Maryland, so unaffiliated voters mostly sit them out.

These are not official turnout figures. They describe the people on the voter list today, not the electorate on election day. Voters who have since moved, died or been removed are missing, so past elections are undercounted, and the older the election, the more is missing. For official turnout, use the State Board of Elections' published results.
