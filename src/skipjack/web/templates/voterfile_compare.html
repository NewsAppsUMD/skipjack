{{ c.earlier_label }} to {{ c.later_label }}

How the statewide voter list changed between two State Board of Elections snapshots, {{ c.earlier }} and {{ c.later }}.

This compares totals, not people. A voter who moved away, died or was removed between the two dates is only in the earlier list, and a voter who registered is only in the later one. The comparison shows how the totals moved. It cannot say who changed party or who left, because that would mean matching individual voters across the two files, and Skipjack publishes only counts.

What this cannot show

  • Who changed party or who left. Totals can move in opposite directions at once. A party's count can hold steady while it loses and gains voters in equal numbers. Skipjack's party switching page counts party changes directly, from the State Board's monthly reports.
  • Participation. The {{ c.earlier }} list has vote history for {{ c.elections.earlier|join(" and ") }} only, and the {{ c.later }} list for {{ c.elections.later|join(", ") }}. {% if not c.elections.shared %}No election is in both, so participation cannot be compared between them.{% endif %}
