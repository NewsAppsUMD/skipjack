Estimated race of Maryland voters

Maryland does not record a voter's race. These figures are estimates, made from each registered voter's name and ZIP code, for the State Board of Elections' statewide voter list as of {{ r.snapshot|day_label }}.

An estimate for a group, never a fact about a person. Each voter's race is a set of probabilities (Bayesian Improved Surname Geocoding, the wru package), built from their surname, first and middle names and the share of each race in their ZIP code. A count here is the sum of those probabilities for a group of voters, rounded. The method is least reliable for Black voters in integrated areas, for multiracial voters, and for small groups.

No individual estimate is published, and counts of 1 to {{ r.suppress_below - 1 }} are hidden. How the estimate works and where it errs.
