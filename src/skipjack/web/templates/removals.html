How to read this

  • The group names are Skipjack's. "Address confirmation mailings" adds the board's "mail verification (NVRA)" and "confirmation mail" categories, the two whose names refer to mailings. The board's reports do not define its categories.
  • The removal figures come from the report's statewide summary table. The inactive and confirmation mailing charts add up the county activity table for the whole state.
