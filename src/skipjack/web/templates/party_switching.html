Voters changing party

Statewide, by month, from the State Board's monthly reports, January 2010 to {{ latest|month_label }}.

Maryland's primaries are closed: a voter can take part only in the primary of the party they are registered with. Voter registration, party changes included, closes 21 days before an election, and voters who are not affiliated with a party may still join one until the day before early voting begins (State Board of Elections). The reports give the party a voter left, not the party they joined.
