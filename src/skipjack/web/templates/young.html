Caveats

  • This is one snapshot, taken {{ y.snapshot_label }}. It does not include anyone who registered after {{ y.snapshot }}, and the part of the year after {{ y.cutoff_label }} has historically held about half of the year's young registrations. The {{ y.year }} numbers are partial, and comparisons cover January 1 to {{ y.cutoff_label }} of each year.
  • The file only includes people who are still registered. Earlier years are undercounted, and two voter lists show by how much. {% if y.shrink %} Between the {{ y.shrink_label }} file and this one, the young registrants of January 1 to {{ y.cutoff_label }} {% for s in y.shrink %}{{ s.year }} went from {{ "{:,}".format(s.was) }} to {{ "{:,}".format(s.now) }} ({{ "%.1f"|format(s.lost_pct) }}% gone){{ ", and those of " if not loop.last else "" }}{% endfor %}. That is only the time between the two files; the loss before the earlier file was made is not visible, so the true undercount is larger. {% else %} A second voter list would be needed to measure it. {% endif %}
  • Party is the voter's party today, not the party they chose when they registered. Someone who registered unaffiliated in 2018 and later joined a party, often to vote in Maryland's closed primaries, appears as a party member. That makes the past look less unaffiliated than it was.
  • "New registrant" means a first-time Maryland registration: the county and state registration dates match, so people who moved between counties are left out. Age is the voter's age on the day they registered, and only registrations at 18 or older count.
  • Voters who pre-registered at 16 or 17 are left out. The youngest recent pre-registrants are not in the file yet, so including them would make {{ y.year }} look artificially low.
  • Small counties are noisy. A change of a few dozen voters can swing their percentages by 10 or 20 points, so counties under {{ y.min_county }} new young registrants in either year are not compared.

The aggregates behind this page are new_registrants.json, new_registrant_weeks.json and age_detail_by_party.json. Counts from 1 to 9 voters are hidden. More on the file is on the voter file page.
