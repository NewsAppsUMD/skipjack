{{ election.name.title() }}: same-day registration

{{ election.name.title() }} turnout. {% if sd.certified %}The State Board's final unofficial same-day report, dated {{ sd.as_of|day_label }}. {% else %}From the State Board's unofficial same-day reports, through {{ sd.as_of|day_label }}.{% endif %}

Maryland lets an eligible resident register and vote on the same day at an early-voting center and, since a 2018 constitutional amendment, at their polling place on election day. A registered voter who has moved can also update their address and vote. The State Board's same-day reports count both groups: new registrants and address changes (voters already registered who changed their address). {% if sd.certified %}In the {{ election.name }}, {{ m.num(sd.new) }} people registered and voted on the same day, {{ m.num(sd.new_election_day) }} of them on election day, and {{ m.num(sd.addrchg) }} registered voters changed their address and voted.{% else %}Through {{ sd.as_of|day_label }}, {{ m.num(sd.new) }} people had registered and voted on the same day and {{ m.num(sd.addrchg) }} registered voters had changed their address and voted.{% endif %}

These are the board's unofficial counts, printed "Unofficial" on every sheet. No certified report counts same-day registration, so this page shows them for past elections too. The sheets say they leave out mail-in voters, and that provisional applications filled in by hand are not included (those printed from the electronic pollbooks are).
