How to read this

  • The group names are Skipjack's. The State Board's own category names changed over the years (for example, two spellings of the elderly and disabled category), and several are folded together here so a group means the same thing in 2010 and today.
  • Duplicate applications, which the reports print in their own column, are not counted.
  • FPCA is the Federal Post Card Application used by military and overseas voters. ERIC is the Electronic Registration Information Center, a multi-state group whose data the board uses to find eligible residents who are not registered; the board's reports label the registrations that follow as "ERIC report".
  • "From another board" is a small category the reports name only by that label. No report since April 2019 shows more than zero for it. Voters who move between counties are recorded as removals from the county they left.
  • Online registrations first appear on the reports in July 2012 and same-day registrations in April 2016, so those charts begin there.
